Alfred 2.0

Personal AI assistant application.

About this app

Alfred 2.0 is a personal assistant application operated by its owner for a single user and his explicitly authorized accounts. It uses Google APIs (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Contacts) solely to help the owner manage his own personal information: reading and drafting his email, viewing and updating his own calendar, and organizing his own documents.

The application is not distributed to the public. The only user who authorizes it is the owner of the Google account it serves.

Privacy Policy

What data is accessed: only the Google account data of the single user who authorizes the app (email, calendar events, documents, spreadsheets, contacts), through OAuth scopes granted by that user.

How data is used: exclusively to provide the assistant functionality the user requests (summaries, scheduling, retrieval, drafts). Data is not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising.

Where data is processed and stored: on the owner's own private server infrastructure. Derived notes may be stored in the owner's private workspace.

Revocation: the user can revoke all access at any time via myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by asking the assistant to revoke it. Revoking the OAuth grant stops all data access immediately.

Contact: for any privacy request or question, contact the developer at alfred.agent.2025@gmail.com.
